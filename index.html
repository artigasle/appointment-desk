Nothing is saved automatically. Closing or reloading clears entered information. No analytics, external fonts, or third-party scripts.

Sign & return

  1. Download and review the PDF.
  2. Attach it to your official email. An email draft cannot attach the PDF automatically.
  3. The commander opens it in desktop Acrobat and signs the certificate field with their CAC.
  4. Save and return the signed original. Do not print to PDF after signing.

A generated field is not a signature. CAC signing and trust validation require testing in your workstation environment. This app does not send mail, validate returned signatures, or track returns.
